# Comprehensive Encyclopedia of 210 Classical Vedic Astrological Sutras

## 1. Introduction & Analytical Framework

Vedic Astrology (*Jyotisha*) relies on precise planetary principles (*Sutras*) that decode human life, karmic patterns, wealth, health, and spiritual evolution. This comprehensive manual compiles **210 authentic principles** categorized by thematic domain. Each principle captures planetary placements, house lordships (*Bhavat Bhavam*), conjunctions (*Yuti*), aspects (*Drishti*), and nakshatra dynamics.

## 2. Section I: Raja Yogas, Royal Status, and Supreme Fortune (Sutras 1–35)

14. **Kendra-Trikona Union**: Combination of a Kendra lord (Lord Vishnu) and Trikona lord (Goddess Lakshmi) forms an indestructible Raja Yoga even if afflicted.
15. **Rahu-Ketu Yogakaraka Status**: Rahu or Ketu placed in Kendra or Trikona conjuncted with a Kendra or Trikona lord functions as a powerful Rajayoga-karaka.
16. **Sakata Exception Rajayoga**: Jupiter in the 5th house and Moon in the 10th house converts 6-8 Sakata Yoga into a benevolent Raja Yoga.
17. **Exalted Planet in Kendra**: An exalted planet positioned in a Kendra house elevating Lagna lord grants royal status.
18. **Vargottama Lagna**: Lagna occupying the same sign in both D1 (Rashi) and D9 (Navamsha) charts ensures lifelong success and protection.
