### 1.1 KP Nakshatra Division System

Zodiac (360°) → 12 Signs → 27 Nakshatras (13°20' each) → 4 Padas each (3°20') → 9 Subs per Nakshatra (proportional to Vimshottari dasha years)

3. Note degree within nakshatra
4. Apply the sub table above sequentially from Ketu
5. Find which sub's cumulative range covers the planet's position within nakshatra

**Event Promise Rule:**

### 1.3 Significator Ranking (4-Tier Hierarchy)

For any house, significators are ranked in THIS order (most powerful first):

```
TIER 1 (Strongest): Planets physically occupying the house
TIER 2: Planets in the nakshatra of Tier 1 planets
TIER 3: Lord of the house (sign-lord of that cusp)
TIER 4: Planets in the nakshatra of the house lord
```

**Why this matters for timing:**
- When Dasha-Antardasha-Pratyantar lords are from Tier 1 or 2 significators → event is near-certain and imminent
- When only Tier 3 or 4 → event possible but timing may slip

**Important refinement — Retrograde planets:**
A retrograde planet in the house → event in that house may come, then reverse, then come again. Not denial — repetition.

**Important refinement — Combust planets:**
A combust planet (within 6° of Sun) as significator → that significator is weakened; event may happen through other significators but that specific planet's domains are suppressed.

### 2.2 Pada Analysis (3°20' each — D-9 Sign Mapping)

Each nakshatra has 4 padas, each mapping to a D-9 (Navamsa) sign in the order of Aries:

```
Pada 1 → Aries Navamsa
Pada 2 → Taurus Navamsa
Pada 3 → Gemini Navamsa
Pada 4 → Cancer Navamsa
(Then continues Aries again for next nakshatra)
```

**Why pada matters in KP-Nadi:**
- A planet in Pada 1 of Pushya (Saturn-ruled nakshatra) is in Aries navamsa — energized, Mars-flavored
- Same planet in Pada 4 of Pushya is in Cancer navamsa — emotional, Moon-flavored
- This shifts HOW the planet delivers its results — same nakshatra, different texture

**Advanced KP Rule — Pada Junction:**
When a planet is within 0°10' of a pada junction, it has qualities of BOTH padas. This creates an unstable energy — good for prediction note but risky for major decisions. Flag this:
"[Planet X] is at a pada junction — its delivery mode is ambiguous. Expect fluctuation in [domains]."

### 3.1 Bhrigu Bindu — The Chart's Most Sensitive Degree

**Calculation:**
```
Bhrigu Bindu (BB) = (Moon's absolute longitude + Rahu's absolute longitude) / 2

Rule: Convert both to absolute degrees (0–360°)
If |Moon − Rahu| > 180°, add 360° to the smaller before averaging

Example:
Moon at 95° (5° Cancer) + Rahu at 240° (0° Sagittarius)
BB = (95 + 240) / 2 = 167.5° = 17°30' Virgo
```

**Identify BB's nakshatra, pada, and KP sub — this adds another level of sensitivity reading.**

**BB Applications:**
1. Any planet natally within 2° of BB → that planet is the chart's primary karmic trigger
2. When any planet transits EXACT BB degree → life-changing event in that planet's domain
3. When MD/AD lord transits BB → peak manifestation event of that dasha period
4. When BOTH dasha lord and a major transiting planet (Guru or Shani) are at BB simultaneously → highest-certainty major life event

**BB in KP:** If BB falls on or within 1° of a cuspal degree → that house's matters will be dramatically triggered when any major planet transits BB.
