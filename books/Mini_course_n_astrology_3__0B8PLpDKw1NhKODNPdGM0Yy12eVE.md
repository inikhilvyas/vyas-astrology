A Horoscope is like a Roadmap

A Horoscope is like a Roadmap, when you come to a cross road in your
life and you are wondering which way do I go which road do I follow!!!
You can then look in the Horoscope for guidance, it will tell you which
energies are available for you to work with.

It will give you an insight to your own potentials and possibilities, it will
tell you in which area of your life you can shine.

However it's up to you to choose, and we do have choices, there is
nothing in Astrology that in any way denies free will of the individual.
Once you have made your choice it's up to you to take responsibility for
yourself. Astrology only points the direction that things are likely to take;
it's a guide to show you what's ahead of you in whatever direction you
choose to follow. When your mind is made up and you have chosen your
path, life and faith will then again take its course.

From birth we create patterns in our lives and these have formed our
personalities over the years. It gives us an excuse not to shine and be the
best that we can be; it stops us from taking responsibility for ourselves.
However the truth that sets us free is an embrace of the "Divine" in us.
The Horoscope can help you to discover who you are, and how to shape
your own destiny, overcome your weaknesses, and develop strengths and
assets, so that you can get the most out of your life.

The Sun travels through the whole horoscope -360 degrees - in 24hours
and stays approximate 2 hours in each House. Every morning between 5-
7 am the Sun is raising on the Ascendant, that is the eastern angle of the
Horoscope, the cusp of the first House.

The Houses are divided into 2 sections and each House is 30 degrees.
Because of the rotation of the earth it causes the Sun, Moon and all the
planets to pass through the !2 Houses of the Horoscope.

Every 4 minutes the sign on the Ascendant move a degree and that is why
it's so important to have the birth time as exact as possible. So when you
draw up the Horoscope, the correct sign and degree will appear on the
Ascendant and all the Houses will be in the right place. It will then
represent the person’s unique planetary picture at the time of Birth. To
predict some future event you also need the exact Birth time.

When the Birth time is not known, the Sun-sign in which you are born is
placed on the cusp of the first House. It is this kind of chart which is
utilized in the forecast printed in the popular Astrology Papers.

THE ELEMENTS

When you study a Horoscope you have to consider it as a whole because
all the planets and aspect work together to form your character. The
Twelve signs of the Zodiac are divided into different elements in groups
of four. There are four elements - Fire - Earth - Air - Water

Air signs are- . Gemini - Libra - Aquarius

Water signs are - Cancer - Scorpio - Pisces

By counting the number of planets in each sign, there are eight planets
plus the Sun and the Moon, you can determine how the persons energy
will flow. People with a balanced amount of planets in each of the
elements are more in harmony with themselves, than those with an
imbalanced amount, like six Fire and one earth and three Air. They will
have more of a Fiery personality.

THE FIRE SIGNS

ARIES LEO SAGITTARIUS

The three Fire signs are Aries-Leo-Sagittarius. When the Sun is in one of
the Fire signs the individual is known to be enthusiastic and idealistic.
These people show confidence, however they are also very sensitive and
because of this they sometimes over-react to a situation.

Fire signs like fire impart action, light and radiance. They are therefore,
related to the qualities of enthusiasm, love, eagerness, passion, courage,
enterprise, individuality, faith and spirituality.

The keynote of the Fire signs is the Self.

This may sound very selfish but it can also refer to one much greater and
sometimes finer person. This can mean a person who are in touch with
his/her inner feelings and has developed his/her inner most powers to
such an extend that he/she stands out as being highly individualized.
He/she is not anxious to show off, but feels confident in him/her and
know he/she has something to offer the world.

Key words that suggest the attributes of Fire signs: Active - Dynamic,
intelligent - energetic - inspirational - original - impulsive - aggressive,
impatient - generally warm outgoing and expressing love.
