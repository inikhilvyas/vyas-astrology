# Domain-Specific Deep Research Modules

Activate the matching module(s) whenever research enters these specialized domains.

---

## Module 1: Law & Jurisprudence (`[LAW]`)

1. **Jurisdiction & Hierarchy First:** Always establish the exact jurisdiction (e.g., India, US Federal/State, EU, UK, International) and rank authorities strictly:
   - *Constitutional Provisions > Enacted Statutes / Codes > Subordinate Legislation / Rules > Binding Precedent (Apex/Appellate Courts) > Persuasive Precedent > Obiter Dicta & Commentary.*
2. **Holding vs. Dicta Separation:** Extract the exact *ratio decidendi* (binding legal principle necessary for the decision) and separate it from *obiter dicta* (incidental remarks).
3. **Temporal & Amendment Check:** Verify whether a statute has been amended, repealed, or replaced (e.g., IPC/CrPC/IEA replaced by BNS/BNSS/BSA in India) and whether a precedent has been distinguished, overruled, or referred to a larger bench.
4. **Dissent & Split Analysis:** Examine dissenting opinions and circuit/High Court splits—dissents often reveal structural vulnerabilities and future doctrinal shifts.
5. **Educational Disclaimer:** Present all legal research strictly for educational and scholarly analysis, never as formal legal representation.

## Module 2: Coding, Systems & Computer Science (`[CODE]`)

1. **Specification & Internals First:** Go beyond tutorial-level API usage. Inspect RFCs, language specifications, compiler/runtime behavior, memory models, OS kernel syscalls, and distributed consensus guarantees.
2. **Complexity & Resource Profiling:** Analyze time complexity, space/memory overhead, cache locality, I/O bottlenecks, lock contention, and tail latency ($p_{99}$ / $p_{99.9}$) rather than average-case toy metrics.
3. **Failure Modes & Edge Cases:** Explicitly investigate concurrency race conditions, network partitions, integer overflows, null/boundary states, backpressure collapse, and security attack surfaces (OWASP, CVEs, side channels).
4. **Benchmark Skepticism:** Audit vendor benchmarks for hardware specs, dataset size, warm-cache bias, and cherry-picked configurations.
5. **Architectural Trade-Off Matrix:** Map every solution across Latency, Throughput, Consistency, Operational Complexity, and Failure Recovery.

## Module 3: Astrology & Classical Esoteric Systems (`[ASTRO]`)

1. **Astronomical vs. Interpretive Separation:** Distinguish physical astronomy/ephemeris calculations (sidereal *Nirayana* with specific *Ayanamsha* like Lahiri/Chitrapaksha vs. tropical *Sayana*, true vs. mean nodes, house systems like Whole Sign, Equal, Placidus, Sripati) from symbolic interpretive frameworks.
2. **Multi-Tradition Triangulation:** Never flatten distinct systems into generic pop-astrology. Explicitly identify and compare frameworks when relevant:
   - **Vedic / Jyotisha:** Parashari (*Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*), Jaimini (*Jaimini Sutras* — Chara Karakas, Rashi Drishti, Chara Dasha), Tajika (*Nilakanthi* — annual Varshaphala, yogas), Nadi traditions, and classical commentaries (*Brihat Jataka*, *Phaladeepika*, *Saravali*, *Uttara Kalamrita*).
   - **Hellenistic & Western Classical:** Dorotheus of Sidon, Vettius Valens, Claudius Ptolemy (*Tetrabiblos*), Firmicus Maternus, and Medieval/Renaissance reception (Bonatti, Lilly).
   - **Modern Psychological / Archetypal:** Dane Rudhyar, Liz Greene, Richard Tarnas.
3. **Multi-Factor Synthesis Rule (No Single-Placement Reductionism):** In classical Jyotisha and Hellenistic traditions, never judge a chart from a single planet or house in isolation. Cross-verify across:
   - *Rashi (D1) + Divisional Charts (Vargas like Navamsha D9, Dashamsha D10)*,
   - *Planetary Dignity (Exaltation, Moolatrikona, Own Sign, Debilitation, Combustion, Retrograde)*,
   - *Shadbala / Ashtakavarga*,
   - *Aspects (Graha & Rashi Drishti), Yogas & Arishta cancellations (Neechabhanga, Vipareeta)*, and
   - *Time-Lord Systems (Vimshottari/Yogini/Chara Dashas + Gochara transits)*.
4. **Handling Contradictory Sutras & Non-Fatalism:** Classical texts frequently contain opposing rules and cancellations (*Apavada*). Surface both the primary rule and its classical exceptions, and frame interpretations strictly as symbolic, historical, and philosophical inquiry rather than deterministic fatalism.

## Module 4: Hard Sciences, Medicine & Engineering (`[SCI]`)

1. **Evidence Pyramid Enforcement:** Separate *in-silico* simulations, *in-vitro* cell studies, animal models, observational cohorts, randomized controlled trials (RCTs), and Cochrane-style systematic meta-analyses.
2. **Quantitative Rigor:** Report absolute risk reduction alongside relative risk, confidence intervals, statistical power, and thermodynamic or physical upper bounds (e.g., Carnot, Shockley-Queisser, Shannon limits).
3. **Replication & Mechanistic Plausibility:** Check whether landmark claims have been independently replicated and whether the proposed mechanism violates established physical/biochemical constraints.

---

## Module 5: Economics, Policy & History (`[ECON/HIST]`)

1. **Primary Archival & Macro Data:** Trace claims to central bank series, census/national accounts, parliamentary debates, treaties, or contemporaneous primary letters/records.
2. **Historiography & Counterfactuals:** Identify the historiographical school or economic model behind an interpretation (e.g., Keynesian, Austrian, Monetarist, Institutionalist) and test against counterfactuals and second-order unintended consequences.
